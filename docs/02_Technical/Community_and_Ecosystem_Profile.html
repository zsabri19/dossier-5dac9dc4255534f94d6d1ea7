Community and ecosystem contribution

This profile describes documented ecosystem-building work relevant to the proposed centre. It does not convert planning, advisory or relationship activity into a claim of incubated or graduated companies.

Experience statement: May 2026 work connected to NASTP Delta included development of governance and operating materials, a cluster map, CRM/intake workflow, startup contact/pipeline material, tenancy pathways and an Oman/GCC concept. These are programme and ecosystem-architecture outputs; they are not represented as a graduated startup portfolio.

Community engagement model proposed

  • Map founders, park residents, academic and industry partners by vertical, cluster and pathway.
  • Offer clear routes for mentor contributions, problem statements, technical review and founder referral.
  • Log each engagement with a contact, purpose, consent/permission and next action.
  • Return aggregate learning to GWALPHA through the quarterly Clarity-to-Pathway brief.
