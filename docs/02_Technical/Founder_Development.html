Founder development · integrated programme element

Leadership, judgement and execution readiness

Founder development is embedded in venture progress. It addresses the decisions, team practices and institutional readiness that allow technical work to become an executable, credible venture.

Development cycle

MomentFounder workEvidence
BaselineFounder and team reflect on role fit, working style, decision habits, execution conditions and development needs. If a Supsindex instrument is used, informed consent, purpose and data handling are explained first.Baseline profile and agreed development priorities.
StabilizeResolve role ambiguity, establish an operating cadence, clarify ownership and decision rights, and choose immediate governance/financial-control actions.30-day plan with owner, evidence and review date for each action.
PracticeApply learning in live company decisions: hiring, customer discovery, product prioritization, conflict, cash discipline, partner engagement and institutional communication.Decision journal, mentor notes and completed experiments.
ReviewRevisit progress at stage gates. Adjust the development plan to the venture’s current constraints.Progress review; unresolved actions; revised development focus.
PrepareRehearse stakeholder conversations and prepare materials for the selected next route.Investor/customer/institutional readiness materials and handover note.

ClarityOS founder prompts

Position: What is the venture’s strongest fit, and what is outside its present capability?
Need: Who has the problem, how is it evidenced, and who can decide to buy or adopt?
System: Which institutional or market context shapes timing, permissions and credibility?
Advantage: What can the park, partner network, research base or regulatory pathway credibly provide?
Pathway: Which next route fits the evidence and maturity?
Action: What will be done next, by whom, by when, and what proves completion?

Measures

At mobilisation, agree a small set of observable measures with GWALPHA: completion of founder plans; action closure; team/governance improvements; validated customer or technical evidence; quality and readiness of partner referrals; and documented progression decisions. Baselines and targets are deliberately left for agreement rather than invented here.

This programme supports founder development; it does not guarantee company success, investment, customer contracts or market entry.
