Part I · Operational profile

Operating governance and delivery cadence

One accountable operating rhythm links founder progress, partner contributions and GWALPHA oversight. The model preserves GWALPHA’s authority over park admission and access while making operator delivery reviewable.

Decision discipline: every gate has a named decision owner, recorded evidence, an outcome and a next action. Workshops and introductions are activities; verified progress is the measure.

Core controls

  • Documented selection criteria, conflicts declarations and decision records.
  • Founder consent and purpose-limited use of assessment and company information.
  • Partner register with named focal point, contribution, timing and evidence of completion.
  • Monthly delivery and cost reporting; quarterly outcome review.
  • Issue log with severity, owner, due date and resolution record.
  • Handover file maintained throughout the contract, not only at exit.
