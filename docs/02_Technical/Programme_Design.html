Part II · Programme design · working submission draft

ClarityOS park-native incubation programme

NASTP Delta Park Lahore · Five mandated technology verticals · 24-month engagement assumption

The programme converts founder potential into evidence-backed venture readiness. It combines disciplined intake, founder and team diagnostics, structured development, technical and market validation, and a decision-led transition into suitable NASTP or external pathways.

Operating principle: clarity before scale. No founder is advanced solely because of a polished pitch, and no partner introduction is treated as a result until the next step and evidence are recorded.

Governance and safeguards

  • GWALPHA retains authority over park access and admission.
  • Assessments are decision support—not a substitute for committee judgment or due process.
  • Founder consent, confidentiality, conflict declarations and controlled access apply to assessment and venture records.
  • Ambassador commission/related-party interests are disclosed; any founder-paid assessment choice, price and consent must be transparent.
  • Investment, grant, pilot, procurement, facility and market-access decisions remain with the relevant independent institution.
  • Targets and cohort size are set with GWALPHA at mobilisation; the proposal makes no guaranteed success-rate claim.

Reporting rhythm

Monthly operations report: intake funnel, stage movement, milestone evidence, partner actions, expenditure against approved cost lines, issues/decisions required, and next-month priorities. Quarterly review: outcomes, pathway quality, portfolio risks, lessons and approved adjustments.

Final staffing names, cohort capacity, detailed calendar, campus layout, service levels and approved measures remain to be completed from verified inputs and contract decisions.
