Engagement, responsibilities and indicative finance

Proposed engagement for one centre at NASTP Delta Park Lahore. Commercial figures remain indicative until verified salaries, tax/GST treatment and final approvals are supplied.

Term and review

Proposed initial term: 24 months from the agreed mobilisation start, with a formal performance and scope review at month 12. Any extension, renewal or scope variation is subject to the definitive agreement.

Support requested

  • Designated, fit-for-purpose space and access to agreed basic utilities.
  • Site security and visitor access coordination under GWALPHA rules.
  • Permissioned introductions to park residents and relevant GWALPHA stakeholders.
  • Any fit-out or infrastructure contribution separately approved and itemized.

Heavy laboratory, GPU, aerospace testing and other specialist facilities are not assumed in the core fee; availability, access and any charges require a separately confirmed arrangement.

Reporting and handover

Monthly report of delivery, cohort stages, partner actions, decisions needed, cost position and upcoming work. The operator maintains a handover file throughout the term; final transfer covers venture records, agreed deliverables, operating manuals and action status in accordance with the contract and confidentiality rules.

Final legal applicant details, commercial values, tax/GST, invoice schedule, service levels and contract dates to be completed from approved records and decisions.
