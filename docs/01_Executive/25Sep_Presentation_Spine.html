Presentation spine

Keep the presentation focused on the operating programme, how it creates value for the park and what decisions are requested. Use evidence already in the submission; do not introduce new partner promises or financial figures.

Slide 1 · 60 seconds

The proposition

We install a structured incubation operating system for NASTP Delta Park Lahore—not a calendar of isolated startup events. The goal is ventures that are better prepared for real institutional, customer and market pathways.

Slide 2 · 90 seconds

Why structure matters

Brilliant founders still need fit, clarity, governance, customer evidence and a credible next move. ClarityOS gives the team a common sequence and makes progression reviewable.

Slide 3 · 90 seconds

Six stages in the venture journey

Intake → Assess → Stabilize → Build → Validate → Ready. Show the evidence at each gate and the decision owner. Explain that assessment informs decisions; it does not decide admission by itself.

Slide 4 · 75 seconds

Five verticals, seven working clusters

Map aerospace, cybersecurity, AI, IT and frontier technologies to the working Delta cluster map. Describe Delta 1–6 plus AI-expansion buildings 7–9 as the planning scope; confirm precise site allocations and facilities with GWALPHA.

Slide 5 · 90 seconds

Partner stack with distinct jobs

Supsindex supports assessment; IKL supports academia and founder leadership; Medad provides an industry/commercial-review interface; Tech Oman supports GCC corridor exploration. No investment, pilot, facility or market entry is guaranteed.

Slide 6 · 75 seconds

Value to the park

Explain the additional quarterly Clarity-to-Pathway brief: aggregated learning about fit, bottlenecks, partner action closure and readiness routes, subject to data protection and agreed access.

Slide 7 · 60 seconds

How delivery is governed

Monthly operations reporting, stage-gate records, clear decision rights, founder consent, conflict disclosure and a handover file maintained from mobilisation.

Slide 8 · 45 seconds

The ask / next decisions

Confirm campus/site allocation, designated GWALPHA decision owners, mobilisation inputs and final contractual scope. Close with the measurable readiness signals agreed jointly at mobilisation—not an invented success-rate guarantee.
