2. Fees, decisions and boundaries

  • Medad will not charge a fee solely for an initial review of a programme referral, as represented in the proposed partner arrangement.
  • No investment, grant, pilot, purchase, procurement, offtake, introduction or commercial contract is promised by this letter.
  • Any investment or commercial engagement is subject to Medad’s independent due diligence, approvals and definitive terms.
  • Any separately chargeable work, travel or service will be disclosed and agreed in writing before it is incurred.
  • “First look” means an opportunity to review a qualified referral; it does not create exclusivity or restrict a venture’s other financing options.
