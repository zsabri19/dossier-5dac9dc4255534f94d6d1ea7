This LOI records IoBM’s collaboration with WiseDezine; neither NASTP nor PAF is a party or signatory, and appointment follows GWALPHA’s EOI process.

On behalf of the Institute of Business Management (IoBM), Karachi, I confirm our intent to collaborate with WiseDezine on the proposed programme. This follows discussions with Prof. Dr. Muhammad Abbas (T.I.), Dean of IoBM’s College of Computer Science & Information Systems (CCSIS). IoBM’s published profile describes Prof. Abbas as an academic and administrator with over 28 years of experience in research, curriculum development and academic leadership. This collaboration also aligns with IoBM’s industry-engagement mission and the work of its Shahjehan Syed Karim Incubation Center (SSKIC).

1. Understanding of the programme

WiseDezine proposes an incubation centre for GWALPHA Tech at NASTP Delta Park Lahore across aerospace, cybersecurity, AI, IT and frontier technologies. The programme combines founder assessment, staged venture development and routes toward customer validation, workshare, tenancy and market readiness.

2. IoBM’s intended role and contributions

ContributionProposed participation
Faculty mentorshipIdentify relevant faculty from business, CCSIS, CESD and related areas to advise founders on business models, market research, pricing, finance and organizational development, subject to availability.
Startup sourcing through SSKICWhere appropriate, share programme information with eligible student/alumni ventures and coordinate referrals under IoBM’s own selection and consent processes.
Founder leadership and research commercializationExplore agreed founder clinics/seminars on leadership, governance and execution; where relevant, provide academic perspective on research translation, commercialization and industry fit.
Engagement modeVirtual participation with periodic in-person sessions in Karachi or Lahore, subject to a calendar agreed in advance.

3. Coordination

Institutional focal point: Prof. Dr. Muhammad Abbas (T.I.), Dean, CCSIS, IoBM. The parties will agree faculty availability, referrals, session calendar, venues, any costs and activity scope before delivery. Participation in SSKIC or IoBM activities follows IoBM’s institutional rules.

4. Non-binding intent and next steps

This letter records IoBM’s intent to participate. Specific scope, scheduling, faculty participation, facility use and any compensation will be set out in a definitive agreement if WiseDezine is appointed by GWALPHA Tech (Pvt) Ltd. It creates no financial or resource obligation and promises no admission, funding, investment or startup outcome.
