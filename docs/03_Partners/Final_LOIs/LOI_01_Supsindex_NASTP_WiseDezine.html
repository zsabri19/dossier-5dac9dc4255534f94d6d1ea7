On behalf of Yanus Solutions Limited Liability Company (Supsindex), I am pleased to confirm our formal commitment to collaborate as the International Assessment and Diagnostic Guardrail Partner for the consortium led by WiseDezine, with Zeeshan Sabri as Crisis-to-Clarity Architect and Author of ClarityOS™.

Supsindex is a scientifically validated psychometric and competency assessment platform specializing in founder de-risking, startup predictability and entrepreneurial diagnostic analytics, validated across 300+ international faculty members and global venture datasets.

Under this programme, Supsindex will provide its proprietary assessment infrastructure to establish a structured, evidence-based founder-filtering and development operating system across all five GWALPHA technology focus areas:
