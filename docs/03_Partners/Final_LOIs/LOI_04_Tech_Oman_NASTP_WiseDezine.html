How the organizations connect: NASTP is Pakistan’s PAF-led national aerospace and technology-park ecosystem; NASTP Delta Park Lahore is the proposed site. NASTP describes GWALPHA Tech (Pvt) Ltd as an SECP-registered technology company managing its park network; GWALPHA is listed at NASTP Alpha / Alpha Techno Square, Rawalpindi. GWALPHA issued EOI-10-1/092025/1/009 to select the incubation operator. WiseDezine is the lead applicant/proposed operator responding. This LOI records Tech Oman’s proposed collaboration with WiseDezine; appointment follows GWALPHA’s process, and neither NASTP nor PAF is a party or signatory.

On behalf of Tech Oman Platform, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman, we confirm our intent to support the GWALPHA incubation programme proposed by WiseDezine for NASTP Delta Park Lahore. Tech Oman’s role is to help develop a practical GCC corridor for ventures that have completed relevant programme readiness steps.

Programme fit: Tech Oman supports market exploration and problem framing. The contribution is distinct from Medad’s industry/commercial review role; a general introduction or corridor discussion is not itself a pilot or market-entry outcome.

This letter records Tech Oman’s intent to support a GCC corridor and industry-problem pathway for the proposed GWALPHA incubation programme at NASTP Delta Park Lahore. Detailed activities are subject to counterpart availability and separate written arrangements. It does not create an unconditional obligation to procure, invest, award a contract or deliver a market outcome.
