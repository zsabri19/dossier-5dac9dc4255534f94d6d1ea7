# Submission Binder Map

This maps the working Opencode documents into the tender’s package. It is an assembly guide; do not include internal forensic/reconciliation notes in the external submission.

## Outer package

1. `05_Evidence_and_Forms/Cover_Letter.html` — export after entering the applicant’s exact registered name and authorized signatory.
2. `05_Evidence_and_Forms/Evidence_Index.html` — update with exact evidence file names and page references.
3. Signed declarations required by the tender, including non-blacklisting, correctness and conflict/interest disclosure.
4. Tender’s original confidentiality agreement completed in applicant’s exact legal name.
5. Applicant registration, authority, tax, financial and capacity evidence.

## Technical envelope

### Part I — applicant and operator

1. `05_Evidence_and_Forms/Applicant_Profile.html` plus registration, ownership, tax, financial statements, client references and personnel evidence.
2. `02_Technical/Community_and_Ecosystem_Profile.html` plus approved evidence for the May 2026 ecosystem work and permissioned references.
3. `02_Technical/Lead_Architect_Capability_Annex.html` as the proposed programme lead’s personal experience annex, with dated CV and evidence references. Keep these personal credentials distinct from WiseDezine’s company history.
4. `02_Technical/Delivery_Team_Structure.html` as the prospective role-based team plan aligned to the cost model; add CVs and availability statements only for confirmed people.
5. `02_Technical/Operating_Model.html` plus approved governance, reporting and handover evidence.

### Part II — one campus schedule

1. `02_Technical/Programme_Design.html`
2. `02_Technical/Founder_Development.html`
3. `02_Technical/Engagement_Parameters.html`
4. `02_Technical/Site_and_Layout_Schedule.html` plus GWALPHA site plan/confirmed room and building inputs.
5. Executed Supsindex evidence and assessment/data-protection terms.
6. Executed IKL evidence and confirmed leadership/facility contribution.
7. Executed Medad evidence and confirmed industry/commercial-review contribution.
8. Executed Tech Oman evidence and corridor/problem-framing contribution, credited distinctly.
9. `02_Technical/Value_Added.html` as the additional park learning-loop proposal.

## Separate indicative financial envelope

1. `04_Commercial/Commercial_Schedule.html`, completed with approved final price, staff costs, tax/GST, invoice terms and assumptions.
2. Dated export of the controlled financial model.
3. Any separately requested support, fit-out/CAPEX and payment assumptions, clearly distinguished from the core operator fee.

## Internal-only material

- `03_Partners/Partner_Forensics_and_ClarityOS_Lens.html` and the four partner briefs.
- `02_Technical/Lead_Architect_Experience_Profile.html` is an internal evidence/claims review; use the concise capability annex in the external technical binder.
- `06_Review_and_Seal/Source_Reconciliation.md`, `Final_Assembly_Checklist.md` and this binder map.
- `01_Executive/25Sep_Presentation_Spine.html` is a presentation aid, not part of the sealed EOI unless requested.

## Export and check

Export final HTML pages to PDF using the same print settings/scale; keep headings and tables intact. Verify every internal link, page number, signature, date, exact entity name and evidence reference against the final assembled documents. Follow the original tender’s packaging and delivery instructions.
