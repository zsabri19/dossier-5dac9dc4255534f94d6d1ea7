Assessment, development, and a pathway are three different jobs. Collapse them into one workshop and brilliance stays in the building. Four parts · one tenure

What sits under the front.

  1. 01

    Supsindex

    Structured founder assessment, where the live terms are signed. The report informs a human decision. It does not admit a company, fund it, or graduate it. A score is one input beside the problem, the team, the customer evidence, and the park’s own judgment.

  2. 02

    ClarityOS

    The development architecture after that evidence. It turns what the assessment and the conversation actually show into roles, decision rights, and the next thirty days. It is not the test, and it is not a piece of software. Stabilise before anyone is asked to scale.

  3. 03

    Industry and academia

    The participants who can connect a venture to a technical test, a customer, or an institutional path. An introduction is an activity until the other side responds and a next step is on the file. Each seat is distinct. None of them is a logo.

  4. 04

    IKL, the front

    The Institute of Knowledge and Leadership, with the University of Management and Technology behind it, is the name the tenure sits under. During the tenure, IKL is invited to orchestrate or create a programme. The journey is governed by the programme architect under that front.

Techniques fail when foundations are weak. A centre without a sequence is a technique.

What NASTP and GWALPHA receive

Four uses. None of them is a logo.

Fit before a place is used

Every venture is tagged to one of the five technologies and a working cluster before capacity is spent. Aerospace, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, information technology, frontier. The cluster names stay working labels until GWALPHA confirms them.

Readiness before exposure

Governance, roles, and customer evidence are in place before a venture is introduced to a buyer, an investor, or a corridor. Interest is not a pilot. A pilot needs the customer’s own yes.

A path the park recognises

Customer validation, a technical test, a sponsored challenge, tenancy, workshare, an investment review, or graduation. None of these is promised. GWALPHA keeps park admission and physical access.

A record, not attendance

Each gate ends in a decision and a named next action. A monthly report and a quarterly view show where ventures are stuck and which decision the park still has to make. Activity is not an outcome.

Two doors · then the same gates

A resident does not start again.

A new venture enters at intake. A team already building inside Delta enters at assessment or stabilization. After that, the gates are the same. The twenty minutes in the room walk this. This page only names it.
