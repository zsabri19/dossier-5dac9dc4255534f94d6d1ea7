Assessment, development, and a pathway are three different jobs. Collapse them into one workshop and brilliance stays in the building. An institution can front the tenure, and during it be invited to orchestrate or create a programme, without becoming the floor. Standing and a sequence are not the same thing. The park needs both.

Partner lens · not a wall of logos

A seat is a job at a gate.

The paper scores international, industry, and academic participation, and it can use that block to break a tie. A logo, or a letter of future intent, scores nothing. Every seat has to answer the same six questions as a venture: where it fits, who needs it, which ecosystem, what institutional advantage is real, what pathway is credible, and what happens next.

What NASTP and GWALPHA receive

Four uses. None of them is a logo.

Fit before a place is used

Every venture is tagged to one of the five technologies and a working cluster before capacity is spent. Aerospace, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, information technology, frontier. The cluster names stay working labels until GWALPHA confirms them.

Readiness before exposure

Governance, roles, and customer evidence are in place before a venture is introduced to a buyer, an investor, or a corridor. Interest is not a pilot. A pilot needs the customer’s own yes.

A path the park recognises

Customer validation, a technical test, a sponsored challenge, tenancy, workshare, an investment review, or graduation. None of these is promised. GWALPHA keeps park admission and physical access.

A record, not attendance

Each gate ends in a decision and a named next action. A monthly report and a quarterly view show where ventures are stuck and which decision the park still has to make. Activity is not an outcome.

Two doors · then the same gates

A resident does not start again.

A new venture enters at intake. A team already building inside Delta enters at assessment or stabilization. After that, the gates are the same. The twenty minutes in the room walk this. This page only names it.
