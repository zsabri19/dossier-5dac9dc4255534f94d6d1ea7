01
Part I & Part II · the proposal

The bid, in one page

The proposal is to operate a park-native incubation system that moves promising founders from initial fit to validated, institutionally placeable ventures — through structured decisions, not a calendar of disconnected events.

ClarityOS turns the park’s five tender verticals and seven working clusters into a visible founder journey: intake → assess → stabilize → build → validate → ready. Every stage has an accountable decision, recorded evidence and a named next action.

GWALPHA retains park access, admission and infrastructure decisions. The operator designs and runs the programme, coordinates the partner network, keeps the record and reports progress. Success is measured through observable production metrics — gate pass quality, pilots live, tenant- or workshare-ready, IR-ready, corridor-shortlisted — never through an unsupported guaranteed success rate.

How the organizations connect. NASTP is Pakistan’s PAF-led national aerospace and technology-park ecosystem. NASTP Delta Park Lahore is the proposed site. NASTP describes GWALPHA Tech (Pvt) Ltd as an SECP-registered technology company managing its park network; GWALPHA issued this EOI to select the incubation operator. WiseDezine is the lead applicant and proposed operator. Neither NASTP nor the PAF is a party to the partner letters, and no award is implied.
Commercial posture. A fixed monthly core fee built from a transparent cost-to-serve stack for one centre. Heavy labs, GPU and aerospace test resources are partner-hosted or separately approved. The GWALPHA ask is deliberately lean: designated space, agreed utilities and access, and permissioned park introductions.

Park value, not generic programming
